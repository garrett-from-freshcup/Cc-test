How to Use (Super Easy!):

  1. Visit a cafe's Square website in your browser
  2. Right-click anywhere on the page and select "View Page Source" (or press Ctrl+U on Windows, Cmd+Option+U on Mac)
  3. Copy everything (Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C on Windows, Cmd+A then Cmd+C on Mac)
  4. Click "Add Cafe" at the top and paste it in the box
  5. Click "Extract Menu" and you're done!
