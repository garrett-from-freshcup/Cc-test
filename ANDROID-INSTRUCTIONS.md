# 📱 How to Use on Your Android Phone

## YES! It works on Android phones with NO app installation needed!

The **CafeScraperStandalone.html** file works perfectly on Android. Here's how to use it:

---

## Step 1: Get the File on Your Phone

Choose whichever method is easiest for you:

### Option A: Download from GitHub (Easiest)
1. Open your phone's browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.)
2. Go to your GitHub repository
3. Find and tap on `CafeScraperStandalone.html`
4. Tap "Raw" or "Download"
5. The file will download to your phone

### Option B: Email It to Yourself
1. On your computer, find `CafeScraperStandalone.html`
2. Email it to yourself as an attachment
3. Open the email on your phone
4. Download the attachment

### Option C: Use Google Drive / Dropbox
1. Upload `CafeScraperStandalone.html` to Google Drive or Dropbox from your computer
2. Open the Drive/Dropbox app on your phone
3. Download the file

### Option D: USB Transfer
1. Connect your phone to your computer with USB cable
2. Copy `CafeScraperStandalone.html` to your phone's Downloads folder
3. Disconnect your phone

---

## Step 2: Open the File

1. Open your phone's **File Manager** app (might be called "Files", "My Files", or "File Manager")
2. Go to your **Downloads** folder
3. Tap on **CafeScraperStandalone.html**
4. It will open in your browser!

**Tip:** You can also bookmark it in your browser so you don't have to find the file each time!

---

## Step 3: Use It!

The tool works just like on a computer, but here's how to add cafes on mobile:

### How to Add a Cafe Menu on Android:

**Method 1: Desktop Mode (Recommended)**

1. Visit the cafe's Square ordering website in Chrome
2. Tap the **three dots** (menu) in the top-right
3. Check **"Desktop site"** - this makes it easier to get the page source
4. Tap the three dots again
5. Tap **"Share"** or look for **"View page source"** option
6. If you see source code, select all and copy
7. Go back to the Cafe Scraper tab
8. Tap "Add Cafe" and paste

**Method 2: Using View Source Apps**

1. Install a free app like **"View Source"** or **"HTML Viewer"** from Google Play Store
2. Visit the cafe's website
3. Use the app to view the page source
4. Copy all the code
5. Go to Cafe Scraper and paste it in the "Add Cafe" section

**Method 3: Use Your Computer for Adding**

1. On your computer, follow the normal process to add cafes
2. The data is saved in the browser
3. But you can still search and browse on your phone!

## Troubleshooting

**Q: File won't open**
- Make sure you tap on it in your File Manager, not a text editor
- Try opening it in Chrome specifically
- Make sure the file ends with `.html` not `.txt`

**Q: Can't view page source on my phone**
- Use Desktop Mode in Chrome (see Method 1 above)
- Or use your computer to add cafes, then just browse/search on your phone
- Or install a "View Source" app from Play Store

**Q: The page looks weird**
- Turn your phone sideways (landscape) for more space
- Zoom in/out with pinch gestures
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet)

**Q: Will it work on iPhone/iPad?**
- YES! The exact same file works on iPhone and iPad too!
- Just follow the same steps
