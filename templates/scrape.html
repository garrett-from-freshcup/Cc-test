Add a Cafe Menu

Enter the URL of a Square ordering website to scrape the menu and add it to your database.

Example: https://square.site/s/example-cafe or similar Square ordering URLs

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Only works with Square ordering websites
  • The URL should be a direct link to the cafe's online menu
  • Scraping may take 10-30 seconds depending on menu size
  • The tool will automatically clean and organize the data
  • If a cafe already exists, it will be updated with new data

💡 Tips

  • Look for URLs that contain "square.site" or "order" in them
  • You can add multiple cafes - just submit this form again with a different URL
  • After adding, visit "View Cafes" to see the imported menu
